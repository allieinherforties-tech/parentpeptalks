The dishes can wait.

It's late, and the sink is full, and somewhere in the back of your mind a voice says a good parent would deal with it tonight.

Here is the thing about the dishes: they keep. Babies don't keep. The tired parent reading this in the dark — that's the keepable one, and you, standing at the sink with the cloth in your hand, are the only one who can put them to bed.

Leave the plate. It will still be there at breakfast, unbothered, the least complicated member of your household. Go to sleep. You're doing better than you think.
