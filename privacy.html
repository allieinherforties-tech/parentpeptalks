Privacy

We keep this note short because the facts are short. Here is everything we collect and why.

Joining the waitlist

When you join the waitlist, we collect your email address, the page you signed up from, the site that sent you here, your browser user agent, and the time. We use all of it to manage the waitlist and to learn which pages parents sign up from.

Signups are handled by a form service that stores them on our behalf. Your record is kept while the waitlist is active. To have it deleted, or to come off the list, write to hello@parentpeptalks.com.

The sleep calculator

The birth date you enter stays in your browser. Nothing about it is stored or sent anywhere.

Email

No emails go out until launch. When emails begin, every note will carry a way to unsubscribe. Your email address is never sold or shared.

Analytics

Our tools measure which pages get visited and which results get opened, using Plausible. Plausible sets no cookies and does not identify anyone.

Say hello

Write to hello@parentpeptalks.com.
